title: "Alex's Adventures in Numberland"
author: "Alex Bellos"
date: 2019-10-04

One of the most interesting non-fiction books I've read in a while. I will say it's put me sleep more than once, but only while I'm reading before bed. A book that certainly made me more interested in numbers. The symmetry and poetry of math is astonishing. It's funny how a book about numbers has made me more interested in math than ~7 years of math classes. Really cool stuff. Highlights [here](/highlights/numberland).
