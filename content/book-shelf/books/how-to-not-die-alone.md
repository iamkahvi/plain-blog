A book about dating, relationships and break-ups. Probably the first self-help book I've read about relationships. Authored by an ex-Googler! Terrible title.

I learned about a few things from this book:

- <a href="https://www.wikiwand.com/en/Attachment_theory#Attachment_patterns" target="_blank">Attachment Styles</a>

- Learning to <a href="https://lifehacker.com/be-a-better-conversationalist-by-supporting-instead-of-1835373108" target="_blank">support rather than shift</a>

- <a href="https://www.wisebrain.org/papers/ChoosingHappiness.pdf" target="_blank">Maximizing vs Satisficing</a> (<a href="https://www.wikiwand.com/en/Satisficing" target="_blank">satisficing</a>)

- <a href="https://www.wikiwand.com/en/Optimal_stopping" target="_blank">Optimal stopping</a> in the context of <a href="https://www.washingtonpost.com/news/wonk/wp/2016/02/16/when-to-stop-dating-and-settle-down-according-to-math/" target="_blank">dating</a>

All four of those things have changed the way I think about relationships. Hopefully for the better!

Here’s a few quotes:

> Here’s the key: It’s fine to have different interests, so long as the time you spend pursuing your favourite activities doesn’t preclude you from investing in the relationship.

> “When choosing a long-term partner, you will inevitably be choosing a particular set of unresolvable problems.”

> Decide, don’t slide.
