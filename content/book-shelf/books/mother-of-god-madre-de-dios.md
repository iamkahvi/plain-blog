This is a book about the Amazon written by a conservationist - Paul Rosolie. This guy loves the jungle. LOVES it.

The amount of incredible things happening in the Amazon jungle makes the book quite engaging. Paul also enjoys doing crazy things - he comes close to dying on several different occasions.

It all makes for a quick and entertaining read.

> The Native American adage that “we do not inherit the earth from our ancestors, we borrow it from our children” is more relevant today than ever.
