What a wonderful cookbook this is.



I've only read through the first "theory" part, but I don't want to read through every recipe in the latter half of the book before writing this blurb.



I love the framing of the four elements. Some takeaways:

\- Don't forget the *acid!*\
\- You basically always want a *sizzle* when you put something in the pan

\- Crispy food is good food

More highlights [here](/highlights/salt_fat_acid_heat). This book was a large part of the inspiration for [making things from scratch](https://www.newsletter.kahvipatel.com/i/194373639/from-scratch).
