I loved this book. It overlaps with [A Visit from the Goon Squad](https://kahvipatel.com/book-shelf#a-visit-from-the-goon-squad). Same universe and a few of the same characters.

This one is also intelligent and quite funny. And emotional in subtle ways. I also like the new technology they introduce (it's not cheesy).

I’m currently reading <a href="https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/34467031-manhattan-beach" target="_blank">Manhattan Beach</a> which Jennifer Egan wrote between Goon Squad and Candy House. It’s sort of a noir thriller and very good.
