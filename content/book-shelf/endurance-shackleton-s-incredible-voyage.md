---
title: "Endurance: Shackleton's Incredible Voyage"
author: "Alfred Lansing"
date: 2024-05-19
---

This book is the foremost account of Ernest Shackleton's 1912 expedition to reach the South Pole. Spoiler alert: *Things did not go according to plan*.

It's a fascinating account of having control over your own destiny. The whole crew was stranded in the most barren landscape on Earth and somehow they managed to escape with no casualties.

The book was first published in 1959 making one of the older books I've read.

> But the sea is a different sort of enemy. Unlike the land, where courage and the simple will to endure can often see a man through, the struggle against the sea is an act of physical combat, and there is no escape. It is a battle against a tireless enemy in which man never actually wins; the most that he can hope for is not to be defeated.
